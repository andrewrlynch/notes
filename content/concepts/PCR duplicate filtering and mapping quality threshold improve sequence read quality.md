title: PCR duplicate filtering and mapping quality threshold improve sequence read
  quality
type: concept
---

Filtering out PCR duplicate reads and reads with mapping qualities lower than 37 reduces noise in copy number analysis by removing low-quality alignments that could contribute to artifactual copy number variations. This quality control step reduces the average read count per sample but improves the reliability of subsequent copy number [[concepts/parameter estimation|parameter estimationestimates]].
