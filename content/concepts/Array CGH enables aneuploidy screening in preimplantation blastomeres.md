title: Array CGH enables aneuploidy screening in preimplantation blastomeres
type: concept

Array CGH can be applied to detect aneuploidies in single blastomeres derived from preimplantation embryos, opening the possibility for [[concepts/aneuploid|aneuploidaneuploidy]] screening and detection of unbalanced translocations during preimplantation genetic diagnosis. This application allows for selection of embryos with appropriate chromosomal constitution prior to implantation. [@le_caignec_single-cell_2006]
