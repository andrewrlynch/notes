[[concepts/chromosomally unstable tumors|Chromosomally unstable tumors]] exhibit increased [[concepts/immune cell infiltration|immune cell infiltration]] compared to chromosomally stable [[concepts/tumors|tumors]]. This enhanced [[concepts/immune surveillance|immune surveillance]] represents a [[concepts/fitness|fitness]] cost of [[concepts/chromosomal instability|chromosomal instability]] that limits [[concepts/tumor|tumor]] growth and [[concepts/malignancy|malignancy]] [[concepts/in vivo|in vivo]]. ## Synthesis
[[concepts/chromosomal instability|Chromosomal instability]] is established to promote [[concepts/immune cell infiltration|immune cell infiltration]] into [[concepts/tumors|tumors]], creating an immunogenic environment that exposes chromosomally unstable [[concepts/cancer|cancer]] cells to [[concepts/immune surveillance|immune surveillance]]. This infiltration occurs because the genomic chaos generated by ongoing [[concepts/chromosome missegregation|chromosome missegregation]] produces heterogeneous neoantigens and stress signals that attract immune cells, as demonstrated both in mouse [[concepts/models|models]] and human cancers. However, for [[concepts/chromosomally unstable tumors|chromosomally unstable tumors]] to achieve [[concepts/malignancy|malignancy]], they must actively suppress this [[concepts/immune response|immune response]] through a conserved mechanism involving [[concepts/stat1 signaling|Stat1 signaling]] inactivation combined with [[concepts/myc activation|Myc activation]], which specifically alleviates the chromosomal instability-induced immune infiltration and allows [[concepts/aneuploid cells|aneuploid cells]] to persist despite their [[concepts/fitness|fitness]] costs. While the initial immunogenic consequences of chromosomal instability and the subsequent requirement for immune evasion are well-established, the precise molecular signals that trigger initial immune cell recruitment and whether all forms of chromosomal instability generate equally immunogenic environments remain areas requiring further investigation.
