title: Published breast cancer signatures not better than random signatures
type: concept

When comparing 47 published [[concepts/breast cancer outcome|breast cancer outcome]] signatures to random gene signatures of identical size, 60% of published signatures were not significantly better at predicting outcome and 23% were worse predictors than the median [[concepts/random signature|random signature]]. This indicates that the predictive power of many published cancer signatures does not exceed what would be expected from random gene selection. [@venet_most_2011]
