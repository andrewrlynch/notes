## Definition
Abnormal growths of tissue composed of cells that have lost normal growth control, characterized by uncontrolled proliferation.

## Synthesis
[[concepts/tumors|Tumors]] are established as complex, heterogeneous ecosystems characterized by uncontrolled [[concepts/cell proliferation|cell proliferation]] driven by accumulated [[concepts/genetic alterations|genetic alterations]], including [[concepts/driver mutations|driver mutations]], [[concepts/copy number|copy number]] changes, and [[concepts/chromosomal instability|chromosomal instability]]. A key mechanistic relationship emerges between chromosomal instability and [[concepts/immune surveillance|immune surveillance]]: [[concepts/chromosomally unstable tumors|chromosomally unstable tumors]] generate immunogenic heterogeneity that attracts increased [[concepts/immune cell infiltration|immune cell infiltration]], representing a [[concepts/fitness|fitness]] cost that [[concepts/aneuploid|aneuploid]] [[concepts/cancer|cancer]] cells must overcome through specific immune evasion strategies, particularly the inactivation of [[concepts/stat1 signaling|Stat1 signaling]] often coupled with increased Myc activity. This evolutionary adaptation is conserved between mouse and human aneuploid cancers, suggesting a fundamental solution to the challenge of immune recognition. Within individual tumors, single-cell analyses reveal extensive [[concepts/intratumoral heterogeneity|intratumoral heterogeneity]] manifesting across spatial and temporal dimensions, with cells exhibiting continuous spectra of [[concepts/stemness|stemness]] states, variable responses to hypoxic conditions, and diverse [[concepts/transcriptional programs|transcriptional programs]] related to [[concepts/oncogenic signaling|oncogenic signaling]]. What remains contested is whether the molecular signatures derived from bulk [[concepts/tumor|tumor]] profiling accurately capture tumor biology, as single-cell resolution exposes these population-level measurements as potentially artifactual averages that obscure the true cellular diversity and subclonal dynamics driving [[concepts/therapy response|therapy response]] and [[concepts/tumor evolution|tumor evolution]].
