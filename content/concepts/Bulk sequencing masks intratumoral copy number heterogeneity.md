title: Bulk sequencing masks intratumoral copy number heterogeneity
type: concept
---

Array CGH and bulk sequencing approaches, which measure average DNA content across millions of cells in a [[concepts/tumor|tumor]] sample, obscure the cell-to-cell variability in chromosome copy numbers and fail to detect individual cell-unique chromosomal aberrations present within the tumor population. Single-cell sequencing is required to reveal the true extent of karyotype heterogeneity. [@bakker_single-cell_2016]
