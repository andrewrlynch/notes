[[concepts/evolution|Evolution]] in asexual populations proceeds through the accumulation of mutations with small individual effects on [[concepts/fitness|fitness]]. This assumption underpins the predictive approach and allows for analysis without requiring species-specific knowledge of [[concepts/mutation|mutation]] phenotypes. [@neher_predicting_2014]
