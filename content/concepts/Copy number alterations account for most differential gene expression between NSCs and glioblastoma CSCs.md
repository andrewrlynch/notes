title: Copy number alterations account for most differential gene expression between
  NSCs and glioblastoma CSCs
type: concept

The differential [[concepts/gene expression|gene expression]] observed between normal neural stem cells and glioblastoma [[concepts/cancer stem cells|cancer stem cells]] is largely explained by copy number alterations. This indicates that the transcriptional differences between these two cell types are primarily driven by underlying chromosomal copy number changes rather than by other regulatory mechanisms. [@zhao_single-cell_2019]
