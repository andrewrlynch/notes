title: Phylogenetic metrics distinguish trees from different generative processes
type: concept

When applied to trees simulated from different stochastic evolutionary models—including birth-death processes with variable rates, Yule models, and Aldous models—the [[concepts/metric|metric]] based on node labels effectively separates and groups trees according to their underlying generative process. This metric outperforms or complements simpler summary statistics like [[concepts/tree imbalance|tree imbalance]] for discriminating between certain evolutionary models. [@colijn_metric_2018]
