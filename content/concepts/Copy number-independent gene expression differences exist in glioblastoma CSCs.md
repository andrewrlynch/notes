title: Copy number-independent gene expression differences exist in glioblastoma CSCs
type: concept
---

Two hundred sixty-nine genes show differential expression in glioblastoma [[concepts/cancer stem cells|cancer stem cells]] relative to normal neural stem cells that is independent of copy number alterations. These genes are differentially expressed despite having no underlying copy number changes, suggesting that transcriptional regulation beyond chromosomal dosage effects contributes to the glioblastoma CSC [[concepts/phenotype|phenotype]].
