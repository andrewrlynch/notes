[[concepts/disseminated tumour cells|Disseminated tumour cells]] can be identified and isolated from [[concepts/mesenchymal tissues|mesenchymal tissues]] by their expression of [[concepts/epithelial markers|epithelial markers]], allowing detection of cancer precursor cells at the stage of [[concepts/minimal residual disease|minimal residual disease]]. These cells represent the precursors of distant [[concepts/metastasis|metastasis]] and can serve as targets for adjuvant therapeutic interventions. [@klein_genetic_2002]
