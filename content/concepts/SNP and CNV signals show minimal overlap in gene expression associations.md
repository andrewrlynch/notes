title: SNP and CNV signals show minimal overlap in gene expression associations
type: concept

The genetic signals from [[concepts/single nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs)|SNPs]] and [[concepts/copy number variations (CNVs)|CNVs]] that associate with [[concepts/gene expression|gene expression]] variation have little overlap, indicating that these two types of [[concepts/genetic variation|genetic variation]] affect gene expression through distinct or non-overlapping mechanisms. This suggests that the variation captured by each type provides independent information about gene expression determinants. [@stranger_relative_2007]
