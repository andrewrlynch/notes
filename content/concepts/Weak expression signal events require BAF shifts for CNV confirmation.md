title: Weak expression signal events require BAF shifts for CNV confirmation
type: concept
---

States 2 and 4 representing heterozygous deletion and one-copy-amplification states are associated with not-so-strong expression signal evidence and therefore must be corroborated with BAF shift signals. Heterozygous deletions and one-copy amplifications must be accompanied by allelic shifts, making BAF confirmation necessary for these intermediate CNV states. [@serin_harmanci_casper_2020]
