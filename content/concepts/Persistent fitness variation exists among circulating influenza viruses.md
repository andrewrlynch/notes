[[concepts/circulating|Circulating]] influenza A/H3N2 viruses exhibit persistent [[concepts/fitness variation|fitness variation]], meaning different lineages have sustained differences in their ability to survive and reproduce. The ability to make informative predictions about future successful strains implies that this [[concepts/fitness|fitness]] variation is maintained over time. [@neher_predicting_2014]
