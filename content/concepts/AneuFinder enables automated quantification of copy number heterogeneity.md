AneuFinder is a newly developed bioinformatic tool that allows fully automated annotation of [[concepts/copy number|copy number]] changes and quantification of copy number variation heterogeneity between individual cells from single-cell sequencing data. This tool enables systematic analysis of intratumor karyotype heterogeneity that would be obscured by traditional bulk measurement approaches. [@bakker_single-cell_2016]
