The acquisition of the ability to proliferate indefinitely ([[concepts/immortalization|immortalization]]) is negatively affected by the presence of an extra copy of certain chromosomes. This indicates that [[concepts/aneuploid|aneuploidaneuploidy]] impacts a cell's capacity to escape [[concepts/replicative senescence|replicative senescence]] and achieve sustained [[concepts/cell proliferation|cell proliferationproliferation]].
