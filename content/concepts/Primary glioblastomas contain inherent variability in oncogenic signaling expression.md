[[concepts/single cell rna seq|Single-cell RNA-seq]] analysis of 430 cells from five primary glioblastomas revealed that these [[concepts/tumors|tumors]] are inherently variable in their expression of diverse [[concepts/transcriptional programs|transcriptional programs]] related to [[concepts/oncogenic signaling|oncogenic signaling]]. This transcriptional heterogeneity reflects the complex nature of [[concepts/tumor cells|tumor cells]] within individual patients and indicates that simple [[concepts/models|models]] do not adequately capture the true diversity of [[concepts/gene expression|gene expression]] patterns driving cancer progression. ## Synthesis
Primary glioblastomas exhibit substantial [[concepts/intratumoral heterogeneity|intratumoral heterogeneity]] in the expression of [[concepts/oncogenic signaling|oncogenic signaling]] pathways and [[concepts/transcriptional programs|transcriptional programs]], as revealed through single-cell RNA sequencing analyses. This inherent variability manifests across multiple functional domains, including [[concepts/hypoxia|hypoxia]]-response [[concepts/gene expression|gene expression]], proliferation programs, and [[concepts/immune response|immune response]] signatures, indicating that individual [[concepts/tumor cells|tumor cells]] within the same glioblastoma experience or respond differently to their [[concepts/microenvironment|microenvironment]]. The mechanistic basis for this heterogeneity appears linked to [[concepts/copy number|copy number]] alterations and genomic instability, with gene expression patterns serving as both a readout of chromosomal architecture and a driver of phenotypic diversity among [[concepts/cancer stem cells|cancer stem cells]] that are transcriptionally distinct from normal neural stem cells. While the existence of this variability is well-established across studies, the specific molecular mechanisms that generate and maintain such heterogeneous [[concepts/oncogenic|oncogenic]] signaling states within individual [[concepts/tumors|tumors]] remain incompletely resolved.
