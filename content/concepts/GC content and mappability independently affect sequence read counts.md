title: GC content and mappability independently affect sequence read counts
type: concept
---

Raw read counts obtained from whole-genome sequencing are systematically affected by both GC content and [[concepts/sequence|sequence]] mappability, which can introduce artifactual variations in copy number profiles. These effects were previously corrected independently in most published analysis methods, though independent correction was effective for many cases but not for all, particularly in formalin-fixed samples. [@scheinin_dna_2014]
