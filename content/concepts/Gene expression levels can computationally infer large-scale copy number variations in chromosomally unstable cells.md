In the absence of simultaneous single-cell DNA and RNA sequencing data, [[concepts/gene expression|gene expression]] levels from single cells can be used to computationally infer large-scale copy number variations by averaging gene expression over large genomic regions and comparing to reference [[concepts/diploid|diploid]] cell gene expression. This computational approach allows prediction of whole chromosome copy number alterations based solely on transcriptional data. [@zhao_single-cell_2019]
