[[concepts/silac|Stable isotope labeling of amino acids in cell culture]] ([[concepts/silac|SILAC]]) combined with [[concepts/liquid chromatography—tandem mass spectrometry|liquid chromatography—tandem mass spectrometry]] ([[concepts/liquid chromatography—tandem mass spectrometry|LC-MS/MS]]) was used to obtain quantitative information for approximately 70–80% of all verified [[concepts/open reading frames|open reading frames]] ([[concepts/open reading frames|ORFs]]) in [[concepts/disomic strains|disomic strains]] relative to [[concepts/wild type cells|wild-type cells]]. This comprehensive approach provides global measurement of [[concepts/proteome|proteome]] changes across 12 different [[concepts/aneuploid yeast|aneuploid yeast]] strains. ## Synthesis
Quantitative proteomics using liquid chromatography-tandem mass spectrometry successfully measures 70-80% of yeast [[concepts/open reading frames|open reading frames]], providing comprehensive coverage of the [[concepts/proteome|proteome]] to assess [[concepts/protein abundance|protein abundance]] across different strains. This high-coverage methodology has proven particularly valuable for studying [[concepts/aneuploid yeast|aneuploid yeast]], where it reveals that proteins encoded by [[concepts/duplicated chromosomes|duplicated chromosomes]] increase approximately twofold in abundance, demonstrating that protein stoichiometry tracks [[concepts/gene dosage|gene dosage]] with high fidelity when [[concepts/chromosomal position|chromosomal position]] is considered. However, the proteome composition measured in these studies may be significantly influenced by [[concepts/growth medium|growth medium]] conditions, as different experimental approaches employ distinct media—synthetic medium for [[concepts/silac|SILAC]] versus rich YEPD medium for TMT analysis—raising unresolved questions about how medium-dependent effects might impact the observed protein abundance patterns in [[concepts/disomic strains|disomic strains]] compared to [[concepts/wild type cells|wild-type cells]].
