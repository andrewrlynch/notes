title: Array CGH replaces metaphase spreads with genomic clones on slide
type: concept

Array CGH differs from metaphase CGH by replacing normal control metaphase chromosome spreads with genomic clones from selected regions of the genome spotted on a slide as target DNA. This modification maintains the comparative hybridization principle while enabling high-resolution analysis and amenability to automation. [@le_caignec_single-cell_2006]
