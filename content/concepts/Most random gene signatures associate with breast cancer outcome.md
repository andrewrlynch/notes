title: Most random gene signatures associate with breast cancer outcome
type: concept

Random [[concepts/gene expression|gene expression]] signatures that bear no relationship to cancer biology are significantly associated with breast cancer disease outcome. The authors found that signatures derived from unrelated biological processes (postprandial laughter, mice social defeat, skin fibroblast localization) all predicted [[concepts/breast cancer outcome|breast cancer outcome]], and that over 90% of random gene signatures containing more than 100 genes achieved [[concepts/statistical significance|statistical significance]] as outcome predictors. [@venet_most_2011]
