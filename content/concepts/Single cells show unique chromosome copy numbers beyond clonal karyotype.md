title: Single cells show unique chromosome copy numbers beyond clonal karyotype
type: concept
---

While some individual cells from Mps1-truncated, p53-null T-ALL [[concepts/lymphomas|lymphomas]] display the clonal karyotype detected by bulk array CGH analysis, most cells harbor additional cell-unique chromosomal aberrations that are not detectable by traditional bulk measurement methods. This demonstrates that single-cell sequencing uncovers the ongoing chromosome instability masked by bulk karyotyping approaches. [@bakker_single-cell_2016]
