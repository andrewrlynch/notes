title: Simultaneous correction of GC content and mappability improves read count accuracy
type: concept
---

Rather than correcting for GC content and mappability independently, simultaneous correction of both factors can provide improved read count profiles, particularly for formalin-fixed samples that contain clearly artifactual variations when corrected independently. This approach is necessary because GC content and mappability interact in their effects on read counts. [@scheinin_dna_2014]
