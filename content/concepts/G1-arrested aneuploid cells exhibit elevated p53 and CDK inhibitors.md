title: G1-arrested aneuploid cells exhibit elevated p53 and CDK inhibitors
type: concept
---

Cells that arrest in G1 following chromosome mis-segregation show elevated levels of p53, p21, and p16 proteins, indicating activation of the DNA damage checkpoint and induction of senescence markers in these arrested cells. This suggests that severe karyotypic abnormalities trigger checkpoint activation and senescence responses. [@santaguida_chromosome_2017]
