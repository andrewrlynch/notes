title: Small primary tumours with minimal residual disease show metastatic relapse
type: concept

Cancer patients with small primary tumours often experience relapse despite receiving local and [[concepts/systemic treatment|systemic treatment]], indicating that metastatic disease can arise from [[concepts/minimal residual disease|minimal residual disease]] at early stages of cancer progression. This phenomenon demonstrates that small tumour size does not preclude the development of [[concepts/disseminated tumour cells|disseminated tumour cells]] capable of future metastatic growth. [@klein_genetic_2002]
