The presence of an extra chromosome copy affects the [[concepts/metabolic properties|metabolic properties]] of mammalian cells. In all four [[concepts/trisomic cells|trisomic cellstrisomic lines]] examined, metabolic properties were altered alongside the impaired [[concepts/cell proliferation|cell proliferationproliferation]], indicating that [[concepts/aneuploid|aneuploidaneuploidy]] disrupts normal cellular metabolic function.
