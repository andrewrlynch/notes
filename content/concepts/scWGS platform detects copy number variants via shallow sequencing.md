The single-cell whole genome sequencing platform uses single-cell sorting by flow cytometry, automated DNA fragmentation, barcoded library preparation, and shallow multiplexed sequencing to detect [[concepts/copy number|copy number]] alterations in individual [[concepts/tumor cells|tumor cells]]. This platform enables comprehensive karyotyping of primary [[concepts/tumor|tumor]] cells without requiring cell division or limitation to specific chromosomes. [@bakker_single-cell_2016]
