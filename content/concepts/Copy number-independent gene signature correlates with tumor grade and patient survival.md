A gene [[concepts/gene expression marker|signature]] derived from genes that are differentially expressed independently of copy number in glioblastoma [[concepts/cancer stem cells|cancer stem cells]] correlates with [[concepts/tumor|tumor]] grade and serves as a prognostic indicator for patient survival. This suggests that the copy number-independent transcriptional changes have functional significance for tumor [[concepts/phenotype|phenotype]] and clinical outcomes. [@zhao_single-cell_2019]
