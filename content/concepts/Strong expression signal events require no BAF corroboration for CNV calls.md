title: Strong expression signal events require no BAF corroboration for CNV calls
type: concept
---

States 0 and 5 in the HMM represent deletion and amplification events that show very high evidence in terms of expression signal with very low or very high expression levels, and do not require an accompanying BAF shift signal to be assigned final deletion and amplification calls. These extreme states can be confidently called based on expression evidence alone. [@serin_harmanci_casper_2020]
