[[concepts/gene expression|Gene expression]] across large genomic regions shows a proportional relationship to whole chromosome copy number in chromosomally unstable [[concepts/cancer stem cells|cancer stem cells]]. This scaling relationship indicates that transcriptional levels directly correspond to the underlying chromosomal copy number alterations present in these cells. [@zhao_single-cell_2019]
